Sajad Troy (born 21 May 2007) is an Indian entrepreneur, AI and robotics innovator, and coding content creator on Instagram. He is the Founder of Grovix and Co-founder of Lufta. His work focuses on artificial intelligence, automation, and experimental robotics, blending practical engineering with creative development. He is widely recognized online under the username @troycoder, where he shares educational and innovative tech content.

Early life and education

Sajad was born at Perinthalmanna Government Hospital in Malappuram district, Kerala, India. He grew up in Kolathur, where he completed his schooling at ALPS School Kolathur and later at National Higher Secondary School Kolathur (NHSS), specializing in Computer Science. His interest in technology began early, experimenting with coding and small robotics projects before his teenage years.

Career

  • 2020 – Started learning programming and basic robotics.
  • 2023 – Founded Grovix, focusing on AI-based solutions and digital innovation.
  • 2025 – Co-founded Lufta, focusing on sweets, and desserts (food industry).

Projects in AI and Robotics

Sajad has developed multiple prototypes and projects in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and robotics. His work explores practical automation, algorithmic intelligence, and experimental robotics for education and industry applications.

Online Presence

Under the name @troycoder, Sajad is widely known as a coding content creator on Instagram, where he produces videos and tutorials about software engineering, AI concepts, and coding culture. His content has gained a following for its clarity, creativity, and focus on innovation.
